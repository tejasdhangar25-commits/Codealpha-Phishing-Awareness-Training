# 🔐 Phishing Awareness Training

**A Cybersecurity Awareness Project | CodeAlpha Internship**

## 📌 About the Project

Phishing Awareness Training is a cybersecurity educational project developed as part of the **CodeAlpha Internship**. It aims to educate users about phishing attacks, fraudulent websites, fake emails, and social engineering techniques.

The project explains how cybercriminals trick users into revealing sensitive information and provides practical guidance to identify, prevent, and report phishing attempts.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand phishing attacks and how they work.
- Identify suspicious and fraudulent emails.
- Recognize fake and malicious websites.
- Understand common social engineering techniques.
- Learn effective ways to prevent phishing attacks.
- Improve cybersecurity awareness and safe online practices.

## 📚 Topics Covered

### 1. Phishing Attacks
Understanding phishing, its common types, and how attackers target victims.

### 2. Fake Emails
Identifying suspicious email addresses, misleading links, urgent messages, and malicious attachments.

### 3. Fraudulent Websites
Recognizing fake login pages, suspicious URLs, and websites designed to steal personal information.

### 4. Social Engineering
Understanding how attackers manipulate people into sharing confidential information or performing unsafe actions.

### 5. Prevention Tips
- Verify the sender's email address.
- Avoid clicking suspicious links.
- Never share passwords or OTPs.
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Use strong and unique passwords.
- Keep software and security tools updated.
- Report suspicious emails and messages.

## 🧠 Knowledge Assessment

The training includes a short quiz to help participants test their understanding of phishing attacks, recognize suspicious activities, and evaluate their cybersecurity awareness.
